        The basics

        1. Press Randomize to roll a number from 1 to 1,000,000.
        2. You get 7 rolls a day. They reset at midnight.
        3. Every roll gets a category and earns achievements. Achievements give you points.

        Categories

        The category shows how special the number's pattern is, from Common up to Impossible. Repeating digits, sequences, palindromes and round numbers rank higher. The Categories list on the main page shows the odds of each one.

        • Common 982,145
        • Cool 482,347 (has 234)
        • Rare 112,232 (two pairs)
        • Insane 123,321 (palindrome)
        • Extreme 500,000
        • Impossible 777,777

        Achievements & points

        There are over 2,000 achievements, for things like hidden codes (has 583 in it), years (has 1999 in it), digit sums, divisibility, primes, palindromes, dates and times. One roll can earn dozens at once. Bigger patterns also earn the smaller ones inside them.

        The rarer an achievement, the more points it's worth. Something half of all numbers have is worth 15 points. Something only one number has is worth 10,000.

        Achievements you earn for the first time are marked NEW. Collect them all in the Achievements tab.
